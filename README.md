# My Favorite Movie: Avengers: Endgame

## Overview
**Avengers: Endgame** is one of my favorite movies because it has an exciting story, amazing action scenes, and emotional moments.

### Main Characters
- Iron Man
- Captain America
- Thor
- Black Widow
- Hulk

## Why I Like It
1. The story is exciting from beginning to end.
2. The characters work together to save the world.
3. The final battle scene is unforgettable.

> "Part of the journey is the end." – Tony Stark

### Rating
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (5/5)

#### Fun Fact
The movie was released in **2019** and became one of the highest-grossing films of all time.

---

**Recommendation:** I highly recommend this movie to anyone who enjoys action, adventure, and superhero films.
